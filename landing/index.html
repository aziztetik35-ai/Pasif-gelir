FAQ

Which files can I upload?

TIA Portal PLC tag table export (.xlsx) and any IO list in .xlsx, .xlsm or .csv with a tag column and an address column. German addresses (E / A) also work.

Do you store my project data?

No. The file is processed in memory and deleted when the package is ready. No AI service receives your data.

Does it replace the safety validation?

No. Safety IO are marked and the protocols contain safety check items. The safety validation according to your validation plan stays your responsibility.

Other PLC brands?

The generic import works with any IO list. Direct exports for Rockwell and CODESYS are on the roadmap.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes, at any time in the customer portal. You keep access until the end of the paid period.
