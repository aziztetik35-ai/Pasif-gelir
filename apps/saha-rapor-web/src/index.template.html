FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need internet?

No. Dictation, photos, signature and PDF work offline. With internet, AI writes a more polished report. Without it, the offline formatter sorts your notes.

Where is my data stored?

Reports, photos and signatures stay on your phone. Only the dictated text is sent to our AI service to write the report. It is not stored there.

Which trades is it for?

HVAC, electrical, elevators, appliance repair, machine maintenance, security systems, plumbing and general service. Any job that ends with “please sign here”.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Subscriptions are managed by the App Store or Google Play. Cancel in your store account settings. Your reports stay on your phone.
